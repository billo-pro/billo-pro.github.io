PRO Boss Fight Strategies

Learn the common strategies to defeat any boss in the game!
Introduction

Boss fights are a crucial part of the PvE end-game, offering players an additional challenge alongside valuable rewards. Defeating a boss rewards players with two forms of in-game currency (Poké Dollars and PvE Coins) as well as rare and, in some cases, exclusive Pokémon. Achieving a three-win streak against any boss also gives players the opportunity to choose from a selection of Pokémon that can either be syncronized or have increased shiny rates.

This guide will focus on strategies and insights for defeating bosses on HARD DIFFICULTY as they offer the best rewards and the most profitable experience. That said, the same teams and strategies should also have high success rates against the same bosses on lower difficulties, but do note that the quality of rewards will decrease. More general information about bosses, tiers, rewards, and PvE coins can be found on the PRO Wiki.

Disclaimer - Even if you follow the strategies outlined in this guide, success is not guaranteed. However, these strategies are designed to maximize your chances of winning against any boss. I am not responsible for bad luck or skill issues that may lead to a loss.
