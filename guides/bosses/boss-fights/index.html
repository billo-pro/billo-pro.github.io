PRO Boss Fight Strategies

Learn the common strategies to defeat any boss in the game!
Introduction

This guide focuses on strategies and team compositions for defeating bosses on HARD MODE, as hard difficulty bosses offer the best rewards and the most profitable experience. The same teams and strategies can generally be used on lower difficulties, although the quality of the rewards will be reduced. For more general information on bosses, including their tiers, rewards, and PvE Coins, refer to the PRO Wiki.

You should already have a suitable boss team prepared before using this guide. If you do not currently have any boss Pokémon prepared you can use the page below to learn more about building one. Once your team is ready, return here to begin the process of defeating bosses.

Don't have a boss team yet? Head to Team Preparation!
Disclaimer - Even if you follow the strategies outlined in this guide, success is not guaranteed. However, these strategies are designed to maximize your chances of winning against any boss. I am not responsible for bad luck or skill issues that may lead to a loss.
