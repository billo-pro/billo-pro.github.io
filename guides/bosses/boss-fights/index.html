Boss Fights

Learn the common strategies to defeat any boss in the game!
Introduction

Boss fights are a crucial part of the PvE end-game, offering players an additional challenge alongside valuable rewards. Defeating a boss rewards players with two forms of in-game currency (Poké Dollars and PvE Coins) as well as rare and, in some cases, exclusive Pokémon.

Achieving a three-win streak against any boss also gives players the opportunity to choose from a selection of Pokémon with either the ability to be nature synchronized or have boosted shiny rates, making boss battles a particularly worthwhile end-game activity.

This guide will focus on strategies and insights for defeating bosses on HARD DIFFICULTY, as they offer the best rewards and the most profitable experience. That said, the same teams and strategies should also have high success rates against the same bosses on lower difficulties.

Disclaimer - Even if you follow the strategies outlined in this guide, success is not guaranteed. However, these strategies are designed to maximize your chances of winning against any boss. I am not responsible for bad luck or skill issues that may lead to a loss.
